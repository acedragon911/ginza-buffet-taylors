CATEGORIES

BBQ

Barbecue is a method and apparatus for cooking food, often meat, with the heat and hot gases of a fire, smoking wood, or hot coals of charcoal. It may also include application of a marinade, spice rub, or basting sauce to the meat. The techniques used in barbecue are hot smoking and smoke cooking.
